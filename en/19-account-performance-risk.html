Core Performance Metrics
Order Defect RateODR
Order Defect Rate, Amazon requires less than 1%. Includes negative feedback, A-to-Z guarantee claims and credit card chargebacks.
Late Shipment RateLSR
Late Shipment Rate, Amazon requires less than 4%. Refers to the percentage of orders not shipped within the promised time.
Pre-Fulfillment Cancel RatePFCR
Pre-fulfillment Cancel Rate, Amazon requires less than 2.5%. Refers to the percentage of orders cancelled by sellers before shipment.
Customer Response TimeCRT
Buyer-Seller Message Response Time, Amazon requires response within 24 hours.
