TM vs RTrademark Status Difference
TM: Trademark application submitted but not yet registered, under review. Can be used for Brand Registry but limited protection, cannot prevent others from using the same mark.
R: Trademark has obtained registration certificate, enjoys full legal protection. Can completely prevent others from using identical or similar marks, Brand Registry unlocks all brand benefits.
Key Differences: R mark can file lawsuits for enforcement, TM can only file oppositions; R mark can use Transparency Program, TM cannot.

Practical Cases
⚠️ Case 1: Unauthorized Product Design Patent Infringement
Situation: Seller sourced products from factory and reached Best Seller status, but didn't obtain design patent authorization from the manufacturer.
Result: Manufacturer filed design patent infringement complaint with Amazon, product Listing forced down. Seller's inventory became obsolete, advertising and promotion costs totaling over $50,000 wasted.
Lesson: Before selling non-own-brand products, verify patent status and obtain written authorization from brand owner or factory to avoid infringement risks.
⚠️ Case 2: Brand Domain Name Hijacked
Situation: Seller registered brand "GreenLife" on Amazon and achieved sales success, but failed to register corresponding domain greenlife.com.
Result: Competitor registered the domain and created counterfeit website selling low-priced fake products, damaging brand reputation. Seller attempted to buy back domain, competitor demanded $30,000.
Lesson: Register related domains (.com, .net, .cn) immediately after brand registration, also secure social media accounts to prevent brand asset encroachment.
⚠️ Case 3: No Brand Registry - Hijacked and Maliciously Reported
Situation: Seller sold hot products without Brand Registry, Listing hijacked by multiple sellers, prices undercut.
Result: Hijacker maliciously reported safety issues with seller's products, Amazon suspended selling privileges. Seller couldn't use brand tools to defend, took 2 months to appeal, store ranking dropped significantly.
Lesson: Complete Brand Registry before product launch to access brand protection tools and combat hijacking and malicious reports.
⚠️ Case 4: TM Registration Cannot Use Transparency Program
Situation: Seller completed Brand Registry with TM mark, product heavily counterfeited, wanted to use Transparency Program to combat fakes.
Result: Transparency Program requires R mark, TM mark ineligible. Seller had to manually report counterfeits with low efficiency.
Lesson: Complete trademark registration to get R mark as soon as possible to unlock all brand protection features.
✅ Case 5: Successful Brand Registry Protection
Situation: Seller completed Brand Registry in advance, encountered hijacking after product launch.
Result: Seller filed one-click complaint through Brand Registry tools, Amazon removed hijacker Listing within 24 hours. Later used A+ pages and Store to enhance brand image, conversion rate increased by 30%.
Tips: Brand Registry is not only a protection tool but also an important means to enhance brand value and conversion.
