1. Introduction

amzrover.cn ("we") is committed to protecting your personal information and privacy rights. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information, and how you control your own information.

This policy applies to all users visiting the amzrover.cn website. We comply with relevant Chinese laws and regulations, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information Collection

2.1 Types of Information We Collect

  • Access log data: IP address, browser type, access time, visited pages
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Location information: Approximate geographic location based on IP address
  • Cookie data: See Cookie Policy section below
  • User feedback: Feedback or inquiry information you submit through the website (if any)

2.2 How We Collect Information

  • Automatic collection: Through website server logs, cookies, and similar technologies
  • Active submission: Feedback or inquiry information you voluntarily submit
  • Third-party sources: We do not purchase or obtain user information from third parties

3. Information Use

3.1 How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve website services: Optimize website content and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic: Understand user access behavior and preferences
  • Security protection: Detect and prevent malicious access, attacks, or abuse
  • Legal compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Communication response: Reply to your inquiries or feedback (if any)

3.2 We Will Not

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Collect sensitive personal information (such as ID numbers, bank card numbers, etc.)

4. Cookie Policy

4.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device, used to remember your preferences and access information.

4.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration Required
Essential Cookies Basic website functionality Session Yes
Analytics Cookies Analyze website access data 24 months No
Preference Cookies Remember your language preference 12 months No

4.3 How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

5. Information Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption: All pages use HTTPS protocol encryption
  • Access control: Restrict access permissions to user data
  • Log monitoring: Monitor abnormal access behavior
  • Regular review: Regularly check and update security measures

5.2 Data Retention

We retain your information only for the necessary period. Server access logs are usually retained for 90 days and automatically deleted after security analysis.

Our website is intended for Amazon sellers, primarily serving adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

If you discover that we may have inadvertently collected children's information, please contact us immediately and we will delete the relevant data as soon as possible.
