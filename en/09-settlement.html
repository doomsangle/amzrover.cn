Financial Management Risks: 20 Common Error Cases
❌ Error 1: No Buffer Funds in Cash Flow Budget
Mistake: Use all funds for purchasing and ads, leave no reserve for unexpected situations.
Consequence: When returns surge, account review, or ad budget overrun, capital chain breaks, unable to operate normally.
Correct: Reserve at least 20%-30% funds as emergency reserve, ensure business runs normally in unexpected situations.
❌ Error 2: Ignoring Reserve in Payment Cycle Calculation
Mistake: Think all payments received after 14 days, ignore Amazon's reserve policy.
Consequence: Actual payment only 70%-80%, budget plan completely off, leading to fund gap.
Correct: Understand reserve ratio (usually 10%-30%), reserve this fund gap in cash flow planning.
❌ Error 3: Ignoring Settlement Cycle Delay
Mistake: Think payment received on order day, don't consider settlement cycle and bank transfer time.
Consequence: Actual payment needs 14 days settlement + 3-5 days bank transfer, fund plan completely misaligned.
Correct: Set payment cycle as 14+5=19 days, fully consider delay in cash flow plan.
❌ Error 4: Peak Season Stock Planning Unreasonable
Mistake: Large stock before peak season, invest all funds, don't consider payment delay during peak season.
Consequence: During peak season Amazon review stricter, reserve ratio may increase, payment delay leads to unable to restock.
Correct: Reserve sufficient funds before peak season, stage stock, avoid investing all funds at once.
❌ Error 5: Ad Spend Mismatch with Payment
Mistake: Set ad budget $100/day, but daily sales only $50, ad spend far exceeds payment.
Consequence: Ad spend accumulates, account balance keeps dropping, eventually funds exhausted unable to operate.
Correct: Ad budget should be within 15%-25% of sales, ensure spend matches payment.
❌ Error 6: Ignoring Return Rate Impact on Payment
Mistake: Calculate payment based on 100% conversion, don't consider return and refund impact on actual income.
Consequence: Actual payment reduced by 5%-10% return rate, profit calculation completely distorted.
Correct: Include return rate in cash flow planning, reserve 5%-10% return loss space.
❌ Error 7: Multi-Account Funds Not Isolated
Mistake: Multiple Amazon accounts share one bank account for receiving funds, funds mixed together.
Consequence: Account association risk increases, once one account under review, all account funds may be frozen.
Correct: Each account uses independent receiving account, funds strictly isolated, reduce association risk.
❌ Error 8: Exchange Rate Risk Not Hedged
Mistake: USD payment directly converted to RMB, don't consider exchange rate fluctuation impact on profit.
Consequence: When exchange rate drops from 6.5 to 7.0, profit shrinks 7.7%, long-term accumulated loss huge.
Correct: Use third-party receiving tools to lock exchange rate, or batch convert when rate favorable, hedge exchange risk.
❌ Error 9: Funds Frozen During Account Review
Mistake: When account enters review period, no reserve funds to continue operation.
Consequence: Funds frozen 30-90 days during review, unable to pay supplier, supply chain breaks.
Correct: Reserve at least 30 days operation funds as review reserve, ensure business runs during review.
❌ Error 10: Supplier Payment Cycle Mismatch with Payment Cycle
Mistake: Supplier requires payment within 30 days, but Amazon payment cycle 14 days + reserve, fund turnover difficult.
Consequence: Supplier payment due but Amazon payment not arrived, must delay payment or borrow high-interest loan.
Correct: Choose suppliers with more flexible payment cycle (60 days), or use credit loan to transition.
❌ Error 11: Not Monitoring Account Balance Warning
Mistake: Never check Amazon account balance, only find insufficient funds when balance negative.
Consequence: When account balance negative, Amazon pauses ads, restricts shipping, business completely stops.
Correct: Monitor account balance daily, set warning line (like balance below $500), add funds timely.
❌ Error 12: New Product Investment Not Considering Trial Cost
Mistake: Invest all funds in new product launch, don't consider product failure risk and trial cost.
Consequence: All funds lost after product fails, unable to test other products, business stalls.
Correct: New product investment within 10%-20% of total funds, reserve funds to test multiple products.
❌ Error 13: FBA Storage Fee Over Budget
Mistake: Large shipment to FBA, don't consider peak season storage fee surge (peak season 3x normal).
Consequence: Peak season storage fee from $0.15/cubic foot to $0.69, profit eaten by storage fee.
Correct: Precisely calculate storage cost before peak season, control inventory turnover, avoid long-term storage fee.
❌ Error 14: Third-Party Payment Account Info Error
Mistake: Payment account bank info filled incorrectly, causing funds unable to deposit normally.
Consequence: Payment returned or frozen, fund deposit delayed 15-30 days, affects cash flow.
Correct: Carefully verify bank info before setting payment account, test small transfer to confirm account correct.
❌ Error 15: Promotion Period Profit Calculation Error
Mistake: Calculate profit at original price during promotion, ignore discount impact on gross margin.
Consequence: After 30% promotion discount, actual gross margin may only 5%, profit completely underestimated.
Correct: Calculate profit at promotion price during promotion, ensure discount cost included in gross margin calculation.
❌ Error 16: Ignoring Tax Impact on Net Profit
Mistake: Only look at gross profit don't consider tax, think profit enough to cover operation cost.
Consequence: VAT, income tax etc. may account for 20%-30% of profit, actual net profit shrinks significantly.
Correct: Reserve tax ratio in profit calculation, ensure net profit enough to cover all costs.
❌ Error 17: Large Purchase Without Contract
Mistake: Large purchase only verbal agreement, no formal contract, supplier doesn't ship after payment.
Consequence: After $50,000 payment supplier delays shipping or quality issues, unable to pursue liability, fund loss.
Correct: Large purchase must sign formal contract, specify delivery time, quality standard, breach liability.
❌ Error 18: Inventory Backlog Funds Cannot Turnover
Mistake: Large inventory purchase, but sales slow, inventory turnover days exceed 60 days.
Consequence: Funds occupied by inventory, unable to turnover purchase new products, business development stalls.
Correct: Control inventory turnover within 30-45 days, clear slow-moving products timely, release funds.
❌ Error 19: Not Analyzing Transaction Report Blind Decision
Mistake: Never download Amazon transaction report, only look at account balance, don't understand fee details.
Consequence: Don't know commission ratio, FBA fee, ad fee etc. specific data, unable to optimize cost.
Correct: Download transaction report monthly, analyze each fee ratio, targeted optimize cost structure.
❌ Error 20: Single Payment Channel Risk Concentration
Mistake: Only use one third-party payment account, no backup payment channel set.
Consequence: When payment account has issue (like account frozen, system failure), all funds unable to deposit.
Correct: Set 2-3 payment channels as backup, ensure alternative when single channel has issue.
