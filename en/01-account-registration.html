Practical Cases
⚠️ Case 1: Business License vs Credit Card Name Mismatch
Situation: Seller registered with Company A's business license (legal rep: Zhang San), but the credit card holder was Li Si (company employee).
Result: Registration rejected with "Credit card info inconsistent with registration entity". Took 2 weeks to prepare new credit card, missing sales season.
Lesson: Credit card holder name must match business license legal representative name, or use corporate account credit card.
⚠️ Case 2: KYC Verification Failed Due to Mismatched Address
Situation: Business license address was "Beijing Chaoyang District xxx Rd 1", but utility bill address was "Beijing Haidian District xxx Rd 2".
Result: KYC failed, account frozen. Required 1 month to resubmit matching address proof, causing operational downtime.
Lesson: All KYC documents must have consistent addresses: business license, utility bills, and ID card.
⚠️ Case 3: Video Verification Failure
Situation: Video verification interrupted 3 times due to network issues, and ID photos were blurry.
Result: Amazon deemed identity suspicious, registration rejected. Required re-preparing documents and waiting for next verification slot.
Lesson: Ensure stable network, prepare clear ID original, practice answering common questions (company name, address, registration purpose).
⚠️ Case 4: Multi-Store Account Association
Situation: Seller used same documents (business license, ID, credit card) for US and EU sites, logged in from same computer and network.
Result: Amazon detected account association, both sites suspended for duplicate account violation. Lost accumulated store weight and inventory.
Lesson: Multi-store operations require independent registration documents, computers, and network IPs to avoid association.
⚠️ Case 5: Address Verification Letter Lost
Situation: Registered address was office building, verification letter signed by front desk and forgotten.
Result: Verification expired, account unable to sell. Required contacting Amazon support to resend, causing 10+ day delay.
Lesson: Choose reliable receiving address to ensure timely receipt of Amazon letters.
✅ Case 6: Successful One-Time Registration
Situation: Seller prepared all documents in advance: business license, legal rep ID, legal rep credit card, utility bills with matching address.
Result: Registration smooth, KYC passed on first attempt, store opened in 3 days.
Tips: Verify document consistency before registration, prepare clear scanned copies, ensure legal rep name and address match across all documents.
