Industry Slang

Traffic/Conversion Category
Traction / Scaling UpTraffic Scaling
Advertising or organic traffic starts growing steadily, sales gradually increase.
Best Seller / Hit ProductHigh Sales Volume
Single product enters high sales volume stable phase, becoming a best-seller in the category.
Product PushingConcentrate Resources
Concentrate manpower, financial resources, and advertising resources to focus on building a single ASIN.
Cold StartNew Product Phase
Initial phase when new product just launched with zero sales and zero reviews.
Data RunningTest & Optimize
Use advertising to test keyword effectiveness and conversion rate, collect data for optimization.
Harvest PeriodProfit Collection
After traffic stabilizes, reduce advertising investment and increase profit margins.
Stockout DeathInventory Crisis
Running out of stock leads to product ranking collapse and significant traffic decline.

High advertising spend but no sales or very low conversion rate.

Controlling advertising cost ratio (ACoS) through optimized advertising strategy.

Reduce cost per click through optimized advertising quality score and bidding strategies.
Ranking KeywordsGrab Rankings
Seize natural ranking positions for core keywords through advertising bids.

First burn money on advertising to build keyword authority and improve natural rankings.
Exact Match Keywords/Broad Keywords/Long-tail KeywordsKeyword Types
Exact match: clear search intent; Broad: high search volume, competitive; Long-tail: low search volume, high conversion.
Drive Organic OrdersBoost Organic Traffic
Increase sales through advertising to boost organic search rankings and organic traffic growth.

Underlying Logic

Click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per click are core metrics for traffic operations.
Capital LanguageROI/Profit/Settlementweek
Return on investment, profit, settlement cycle are core metrics for fund management.
Risk Control LanguageODR/Account Association/Second Review/Account Ban
