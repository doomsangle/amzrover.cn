I. Data Metrics

Q1: needfocus onwhichsomeCore Data Metrics?
A: Core Data Metrics:

1. Traffic Metrics:
- Impressions(Impressions): Number of times product is displayed
- Clicks(Clicks): Number of times product is clicked
- Click-Through Rate(CTR): Clicks/Impressions × 100%

2. Conversion Metrics:
- Orders(Orders): Number of completed orders
- Conversion Rate(CVR): Orders/Clicks × 100%

3. Ad Metrics:
- ACoS: AdvertisingSpend/AdvertisingSales × 100%
- CPC: allCost per Click
- ROI: AdvertisingReturn on Investment

4. Financial Metrics:
- Sales(Sales): Total Sales
- Profit(Profit): Total profit
- Profit Margin(Profit Margin): Profit/Sales × 100%

Related Terms: ACoS | Conversion Rate
