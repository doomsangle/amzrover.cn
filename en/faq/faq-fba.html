I. FBAfoundation

Q1: FBAandFBMhaswhatDifferences?
A: FBAandFBMmainDifferencesifbelow:

FBA(Fulfillment by Amazon):
- Amazon handles Warehousing, packing, shipping, customer service and Returns
- Product receives Prime badge
- Fast delivery(2-day delivery)
- Easier to win Buy Box
- Storage Fee and Fulfillment Fee required

FBM(Fulfillment by Merchant):
- Seller handles Warehousing, packing, shipping, customer service and Returns
- No Prime badge
- Slow delivery(7-15days)
- Low chance of winning Buy Box
- No Storage Fee, but international shipping costs apply

Related Terms: FBA | FBM
