Amazon seller management backend where sellers conduct all store operations including product listing, order management, advertising, viewing sales data, etc.

Individual SellerIndividual Seller

Abbreviation for 'Know Your Customer', Amazon's identity and qualification review for sellers, requiring company business license, legal representative ID, bank account information, etc.
GST/HST/PSTCanadian Taxation
Canada's sales tax system: GST (Goods and Services Tax), HST (Harmonized Sales Tax), PST (Provincial Sales Tax).
Brand Registry Related
Brand RegistryBrand Registry
Amazon's Brand Registration program. After registration, sellers can access brand protection tools, A+ page permissions, Brand Analytics, etc., to prevent hijacking and infringement.
TrademarkTrademark
Prerequisite for Brand Registry, requires trademark registration in target market countries/regions (e.g., USPTO in the US, EUIPO in the EU), including text marks, graphic marks, etc.

Advanced detail page feature exclusive to Brand Registry sellers, supports adding illustrated product descriptions, comparison tables, brand stories, etc., to improve conversion rate.
Amazon StoresAmazon Flagship Store
Brand-exclusive pages that Brand Registry sellers can create, similar to independent sites, showcasing brand stories and product lines to enhance brand image and customer loyalty.
Transparency ProgramTransparency Program
Amazon's Brand Protection program that uses unique code labels on products to combat counterfeits and ensure consumers purchase authentic goods.

CPSC Compliance Guide (2024-2025 Priority Focus)
CPSCConsumer Product Safety Commission
U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission is a federal government agency responsible for developing and enforcing consumer product safety standards to protect consumers from unreasonable product injury risks.Priority Focus: Children's Products, Electronics, Household Items
CPSC JurisdictionScope of Regulation
Products requiring CPSC compliance certification before listing:

1. Children's Products (under 12): Toys, children's clothing, children's furniture, children's items (backpacks, water bottles), children's jewelry, children's art supplies
2. Electronic Products: Electric toys, charging devices, home appliances, lighting fixtures
3. Household Items: Furniture, mattresses, carpets, decorations
4. Sports Equipment: Athletic gear, fitness equipment

Note: 2024-2025 CPSC increased inspections on Chinese cross-border e-commerce products. Violations face delisting, fines, or account suspension.
CPSC Compliance ProcessHow to Comply
Complete compliance steps:

Step 1: Product Classification
Confirm whether product falls under CPSC jurisdiction. Children's products (for use under 12) MUST comply.

Step 2: Identify Applicable Standards
Check CPSC website for applicable standards (e.g., ASTM F963 for toys, 16 CFR Part 1500).

Step 3: Third-Party Laboratory Testing
Send samples to CPSC-accepted third-party laboratories (SGS, TÜV, Intertek).

Step 4: Obtain Test Reports (GCC/CPC)
- General Conformity Certificate (GCC): For adult products
- Children's Product Certificate (CPC): For children's products, must be issued by CPSC-accepted lab

Step 5: Register CPSC Tracking Label
Children's products must have permanent tracking labels on product and packaging (production date, batch, manufacturer info).

Step 6: Upload Compliance Documents
Submit compliance documents in Amazon Seller Central, wait for approval before listing.
CPSC Violation CasesReal Cases
Case 1: Children's Toy Lead超标 (2024)
Chinese seller sold plastic children's toys on Amazon without CPSIA testing. CPSC inspection found lead content 1000ppm (limit 100ppm). Product forcibly delisted, store frozen, fine $100,000.

Case 2: Children's Sleepwear Flammability Test Failure (2024)
Seller sold children's sleepwear without 16 CFR Part 1630 flammability test. CPSC deemed serious safety hazard, recalled all products, seller bore return costs and legal liability.

Case 3: Electric Toy Battery Overheating (2025)
Electric toy caused overheating due to battery design defect. CPSC received multiple consumer complaints and investigated, found no UL standard testing. Product recalled, seller faces criminal investigation.

Lesson: Complete compliance testing before listing, otherwise consequences are extremely severe.
CPSC Problem ResolutionHow to Resolve
Scenario 1: Received CPSC Warning Email
Amazon notifies seller CPSC requests compliance documents, usually 72-hour response time.
Resolution Steps:
1. Immediately stop selling related products
2. Contact third-party lab for urgent testing
3. Submit compliance proof within deadline
4. If unable to meet deadline, request extension with reasonable explanation

Scenario 2: Product Already Delisted
Resolution Steps:
1. Confirm delisting reason (email specifies violation)
2. Implement corrections (material replacement, design modification)
3. Resend samples for testing
4. Submit correction proof and new compliance documents for restoration

Scenario 3: Facing Fines or Legal Action
Resolution Steps:
1. Immediately hire professional CPSC compliance attorney
2. Cooperate with CPSC investigation, provide all relevant documents
3. Develop remediation plan and timeline
4. Apply for fine reduction (first violation, good remediation attitude)
CPSC Solution ProvidersWho Can Help
1. CPSC-Accepted Third-Party Testing Laboratories
Provide product testing and compliance certificate issuance, first step for compliance.
Recommended Labs:
- SGS (world's largest testing agency, CPSC-accepted)
- TÜV (German certification body, CPSC-accepted)
- Intertek (UK testing agency, CPSC-accepted)
- UL (US safety testing lab, electronics authority)
- Domestic qualified labs (confirm CPSC acceptance list)

2. CPSC Compliance Consultants
Professional consultants help sellers interpret regulations, determine applicable standards, develop compliance plans.
Services: Regulation interpretation, standard confirmation, testing plan, document preparation, Amazon submission guidance

3. CPSC Compliance Attorneys
When facing CPSC investigation, fines, legal action, professional attorney intervention needed.
Services: Legal defense, fine reduction applications, recall handling, criminal investigation response

4. Amazon Compliance Service Team
Amazon provides compliance consultation and document submission guidance, submit inquiries in Seller Central.
Note: Amazon compliance team doesn't provide legal advice, complex issues need external experts.

CPSC Best PracticesPrevention Better Than Cure
Pre-Launch Checklist:

1. ✅ Confirm product falls under CPSC jurisdiction
2. ✅ Check CPSC website for applicable standards
3. ✅ Select CPSC-accepted laboratory for testing
4. ✅ Obtain GCC or CPC certificate
5. ✅ Product and packaging marked with tracking labels (children's products)
6. ✅ Submit compliance documents in Amazon Seller Central
7. ✅ Regular re-testing (every 6-12 months or when product changes)

Ongoing Compliance Maintenance:

1. ⚠️ Re-test when product material changes
2. ⚠️ Re-test when supplier changes
3. ⚠️ Evaluate compliance impact when design modifies
4. ⚠️ Monitor CPSC regulation updates (check at least annually)
5. ⚠️ Maintain all compliance documents for at least 5 years

Important Reminder: Compliance is not a one-time task, but a continuous responsibility.
