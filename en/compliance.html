Amazon Certification & ComplianceGlossary

Covers store registration, brand registry, tax certification, product compliance and other core concepts

← Back to Home
Store Registration Related
Amazon Seller CentralSeller Central
Amazon seller management backend where sellers conduct all store operations including product listing, order management, advertising, viewing sales data, etc.
Professional SellerProfessional Seller
One type of Amazon seller account that requires a fixed monthly fee (e.g., $39.99/month for US marketplace), can create unlimited listings, and enjoys more advanced features and sales tools.
Individual SellerIndividual Seller
One type of Amazon seller account with no monthly fee, charged per item ($0.99/item), limited functionality, suitable for beginners or small-volume sales.
KYC CertificationIdentity Verification
Abbreviation for 'Know Your Customer', Amazon's identity and qualification review for sellers, requiring company business license, legal representative ID, bank account information, etc.
GST/HST/PSTCanadian Taxation
Canada's sales tax system: GST (Goods and Services Tax), HST (Harmonized Sales Tax), PST (Provincial Sales Tax).
Brand Registry Related
Brand RegistryBrand Registry
Amazon's Brand Registration program. After registration, sellers can access brand protection tools, A+ page permissions, Brand Analytics, etc., to prevent hijacking and infringement.
TrademarkTrademark
Prerequisite for Brand Registry, requires trademark registration in target market countries/regions (e.g., USPTO in the US, EUIPO in the EU), including text marks, graphic marks, etc.
A+ ContentA+ Page
Advanced detail page feature exclusive to Brand Registry sellers, supports adding illustrated product descriptions, comparison tables, brand stories, etc., to improve conversion rate.
Amazon StoresAmazon Flagship Store
Brand-exclusive pages that Brand Registry sellers can create, similar to independent sites, showcasing brand stories and product lines to enhance brand image and customer loyalty.
Transparency ProgramTransparency Program
Amazon's Brand Protection program that uses unique code labels on products to combat counterfeits and ensure consumers purchase authentic goods.
Product Compliance Certification
FCC CertificationUSA
Federal Communications Commission (FCC) electromagnetic compatibility certification for electronic products, ensuring products do not interfere with radio communications.
FDA CertificationUSA
U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) certification for food, drugs, medical devices, cosmetics and other products, ensuring product safety compliance.
CE CertificationEU
European product safety certification mark indicating the product meets EU directive requirements, necessary for entering the EU market.
RoHS CertificationEU
EU directive restricting the use of certain hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment, limiting lead, mercury, cadmium and other hazardous materials.
CPSIA CertificationUS Children
U.S. Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act, safety standards for children's products including lead content limits, phthalate restrictions, etc.
Proposition 65California Proposition 65
California regulation requiring warning labels on products that may contain carcinogenic or reproductively toxic chemicals.
Tax Compliance
VATValue Added Tax
Value Added Tax, VAT in EU countries. Sellers must register for a VAT number in the country where sales occur and file and pay on time.
IOSSEU Import
Import One Stop Shop, EU one-stop import service allowing non-EU sellers to complete VAT filing and payment for all EU countries at once.
OSSEU Distance Selling
One Stop Shop, EU one-stop service for VAT filing applicable to EU-based sellers making distance sales across multiple countries.
EPRProducer Responsibility
Extended Producer Responsibility, EU extended producer responsibility system requiring companies to be responsible for products throughout their lifecycle, including packaging recycling, electronics recycling, etc.
Payment & Collection
WorldFirstWorldFirst
Third-party payment tool officially recommended by Amazon, supports multi-currency collection with low fees and fast settlement, can directly bind to domestic bank accounts for withdrawal.
PayoneerPayoneer
International payment platform supporting multi-platform collection, provides virtual bank accounts for sellers to receive payments from Amazon and other platforms.
P CardPayoneer Abbreviation
Abbreviation for Payoneer card, one of the commonly used payment tools by sellers, supports global multi-currency collection and withdrawal.
PingPongPingPong Payment
China leading cross-border payment platform supporting Amazon, eBay and other platforms with transparent fees and secure funds.
TransferwiseWise
Now renamed to Wise, providing low-cost international remittance services with multi-currency accounts, suitable for cross-border fund transfers.
